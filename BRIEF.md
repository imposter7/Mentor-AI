# Mentor AI — Project Brief

## 1. The Contract (I/O)

**Input:** Your resume (PDF, image, or text), what job you want (text), and your spoken answers in a mock interview.

**Output:** The 5 most relevant live job posts with an explanation of why they fit you, a prioritized study plan for the job you pick, a spoken 7-question mock interview, and a 1–10 rubric review of every answer.

## 2. The Core Experience

A senior interviewer from the company you just saved asks you a question out loud — grounded in questions real candidates reported, the job description, and your resume — and you answer naturally by voice.

## 3. Scope Discipline (Non-Goals)

No logins or accounts, no cloud database, no auto-apply or cover letters, no resume rewriting, no video or body-language feedback, no comparing attempts over time, and no scraping behind login walls.

## 4. Signature Detail

The agent rail: four status chips in the header — Summary, Web Search, Mock Interview, and Review — that display each agent's readiness and pulse in real time while that agent works in the foreground or background.
